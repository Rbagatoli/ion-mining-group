Your statements

Figures are taken from the meter at your site. Where a period is not fully covered by meter readings, the gap is shown as a gap — nothing is estimated or filled in. Statements are frozen when issued and do not change afterwards; a later correction appears as an adjustment on the next statement, so you can always see why a figure differs.
