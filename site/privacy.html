Privacy

What this site
collects about you.

Almost nothing. This page says exactly what, and names the two outside services one page here talks to, because a policy that leaves those out is not a policy.

Covers ionmininggroup.com only. The Ion Mining Group application is a separate product with its own privacy policy, which is the one to read if you hold an account.

No accounts, no cookies

This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics, and has no login. Nothing is stored in your browser except what you type into the calculator, which never leaves it.

Forms open your mail client

The contact and site-submission forms do not transmit anything to us. Pressing send composes a draft in your own mail application, addressed to us. Nothing reaches us until you send it yourself, and you can see exactly what it says first.

Hosting

Served as static files by GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs including visitor IP addresses. We do not have access to them and do not receive a copy.

The two outside services.

The mining calculator asks two public services for today's figures each time it loads, so it does not open on stale numbers. Your browser contacts them directly, which means your IP address reaches them. Nothing about you is sent, and no request identifies you as a visitor to this site.

Coinbase

The current bitcoin price, from their public price endpoint. No key, no account, no payload — a bare read.

Blockchain.info

The current bitcoin network difficulty, the same way. Also a bare read.

If you would rather not

Block scripts for this page, or use the calculator with JavaScript disabled. It will show the figures it shipped with instead, dated on the page, and everything else still works.

Questions.

Write to hello@ionmininggroup.com. If you have asked us something by email and would like it deleted, say so and we will.
