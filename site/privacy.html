Privacy

What this site
collects about you.

How your enquiries, order details and browser preferences are handled, and which services process them.

Covers protonminingco.com only. The Proton Mining application is a separate product with its own privacy policy, which is the one to read if you hold an account.

Public pages and browser storage

The public service pages require no account and use no advertising trackers. Calculator, order and display preferences may be stored in your browser. Private intake stores only a retry key, a one-way brief fingerprint and a receipt reference in session storage; it does not save your enquiry text or contact details there. The client portal and CRM have separate sign-in.

Private enquiries and email fallback

When secure Energy Sites intake is connected, submitting sends your contact details and brief to Proton’s private request service. A receipt is shown only after durable storage. We use the record to assess and respond to your enquiry, and record its source and qualification status. Referral campaign tags and the referring hostname may accompany the request; we do not run a separate visitor-tracking service. General contact forms and the email fallback open a draft in your mail application; you must send it yourself.

Hosting

The public website is served by GitHub Pages. Requests reach GitHub infrastructure, including your IP address; its processing is covered by the GitHub privacy statement.

The outside services.

Some of these your browser contacts directly, which means your IP address reaches them. Others receive requests from the order service. Which services are used depends on the page and the actions you choose.

Your browser contacts these directly

Coinbase

The current bitcoin price, from their public price endpoint, on the calculator, the hardware catalogue, and when you open Build your mine. These requests do not send your calculator or builder inputs.

Blockchain.info

The current bitcoin network difficulty, from the calculator, hardware catalogue, and Build your mine, in the same way. Also a bare read.

Cloudflare

Our order service and, when connected, private Energy Sites intake run there. Your submitted details reach the relevant service, along with your IP address. Intake uses a short-lived keyed rate-limit identifier to limit abuse. Enquiry records are private and restricted to explicitly authorized Proton accounts. Cloudflare hosts the service; these requests are not public listings.

Stripe

Only if you choose to pay by card through an enabled checkout. Stripe hosts the payment page and receives your card details. Proton receives payment status and transaction details needed to reconcile the order; our checkout does not collect your card number.

We contact these for you

The order service requests the invoice. The payment provider also processes information associated with the payment you make.

Strike

Creates the bitcoin invoice when that payment option is enabled. The order service requests it and returns the payment instructions to your browser. Strike processes the invoice and resulting payment.

If you would rather not

You can read the public pages with JavaScript disabled and email sales@protonminingco.com directly. Interactive calculations, model browsing and form-generated email drafts need JavaScript. When market-data requests fail, the calculator labels its dated fallback inputs.

Questions.

Write to sales@protonminingco.com. If you have asked us something by email and would like it deleted, say so and we will.
