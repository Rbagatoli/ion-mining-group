Questions

Things people ask before signing.

Who holds the bitcoin?

You do. Your machines mine to your pool account under your credentials, and the pool pays your wallet directly. Proton never takes custody of mined bitcoin at any point, and we do not need your withdrawal keys to run your fleet. The only access we ask for is a read-only pool API key so reporting can reconcile.

What happens when a machine fails?

Alerting fires the moment a unit drops below its expected hashrate or overheats. On-site technicians work the standard path — reboot, reseat, swap the fan or PSU, then swap the hashboard from spares. If a unit needs a manufacturer RMA, we pull it, document it, and coordinate with you on the return. Labour is included; parts are billed at cost.

Do I pay for power when the site is curtailed?

No. Billing is on metered draw, so if the machines are off, there is nothing to bill. Curtailment events appear on your statement with start time, duration, and cause, so downtime always reconciles against your pool numbers.

Why is your power cheaper than the grid?

Because at most of our sites there is no grid to buy from. We generate on site from gas that would otherwise be flared or vented, or we sit behind a meter where the alternative is curtailment. That energy has almost no competing buyer, which is what makes the rate work — and it is also why our sites are interruptible by design.
