2. Evidence key and source log

Label the evidence

For each answer choose C — Confirmed (the specific fact is supported by reviewed, relevant evidence); R — Reported (an attributed statement awaiting corroboration); U — Unknown; X — Conflict; or N/A (give a reason). A model remains an estimate. Keep facts with different statuses separate.

Next to each answer, record a source link/document, issuer, original measurement or effective date, and date checked. An old measurement stays old when read today. Use an attached evidence log if needed; number its entries to match the checks.

4. Rights and infrastructure · checks 7–12

Rights, infrastructure and remaining work

Checks 7 to 12: rights, infrastructure and remaining work
CheckRecord or ask forStatus / evidence reference
7. Is there a real commercial opening?Attributed owner interest; who controls land, fuel/water, power and delivery access; marketing authority; existing contracts, allocations and exclusivity. Record the actual offer or next authority question. A public contact alone proves no willingness.Status: Ref:
8. Which equipment can you actually use?Generation/conversion, transformers, switchgear/protection, voltage, metering and connection configuration. Request ratings, condition/inspection evidence, use rights and missing works. Record compatibility questions; nearby infrastructure alone proves no connection capacity.Status: Ref:
9. Can the physical site support deployment?Usable pad/building area, truck/winter access, communications and cooling/water where relevant. Record zoning, permits, noise/environmental restrictions and approval status. Maps/photos help locate features; date them and confirm condition or availability separately.Status: Ref:
10. What remains to be funded, owned and operated?Itemized repairs, construction, connection, freight, installation and startup cash; dated quote scopes/exclusions. Name the proposed funder, owner and operator of each item; identify funding evidence and conditions. Keep research fees separate from project capital.Status: Ref:
11. What terms and timing are actually offered?Supply term, start date, approval/procurement route, deadlines and written conditions. List permits, equipment, construction and energization dependencies. Distinguish a requested date from an evidenced schedule or contractual commitment.Status: Ref:
12. Does the evidence meet your must-haves?Check load, location/source, delivered cost, capital, readiness, reliability and timing against the exact brief. List hard mismatches, conflicting claims and missing essentials. Preserve the reasons a site was already known or rejected.Status: Ref:

Choose the next step

□Proceed with the next check: no evidenced hard mismatch; one useful question has a reachable answer source and a finite effort limit. This means further investigation only.

□Park: a material answer, authority route or funding prerequisite is missing/conflicting, or the agreed research effort is exhausted. Record what new evidence would justify reopening.

□Exclude: evidence contradicts a hard requirement. Record the source and reason; reconsider only after relevant evidence or requirements change.
