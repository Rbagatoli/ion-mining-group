Research coverage, not promised inventory.

Your source and location preferences define the search. New source categories require dedicated research; they are not ready-to-use inventory. Marine resources, including tidal and wave, can be considered where relevant. Storage shifts energy and must have an underlying supply.

What we check for each energy route

Generation capacity, output and power available to a new customer are different measures. We check source-specific constraints against EIA guidance, DOE hydropower guidance and DOE large-load rate research. Every potential site still needs its own owner, technical and commercial checks.

Know what has been confirmed.

Public records can identify a lead. They do not establish spare power, access rights or a site for sale. We distinguish reported facts, estimates and owner-confirmed information, and show missing evidence alongside the comparison. Coverage depends on the source and record date; no search establishes that every U.S. opportunity is known. A site is secured only when the required agreements are in place.

Before you start

A few useful distinctions.

Which energy sources can I request?

Hydro, nuclear, wind, solar, geothermal, natural gas generation, landfill and flare gas, biomass or biogas, waste-to-energy, coal, oil, industrial surplus and grid supply. You can also describe storage-backed, hybrid or other proposals. We agree a realistic research scope for your location and scale before starting; selecting a source does not mean a site is already available.

What does the service cost?

We quote the research scope before work begins, based on your region, power requirement and depth of diligence. The scope identifies the deliverables, fee and any further work requiring separate agreement.

Will I receive a guaranteed power supply?

The starting service delivers a researched shortlist and a route to qualification. Site owners or energy rights holders must confirm willingness, net available power, terms and access. Research findings and preliminary capital ranges are kept separate from confirmed offers and contractor quotes.

Do I have to host with Proton?

No. Site sourcing is available for your own mining or hosting operation. For machines purchased from Proton, our hosting service is a separate option.

Are these sites already available or under contract?

A shortlist may include opportunities identified from public records and research. We state the confirmation level for each one. Owner interest, energy availability, access and commercial terms must be checked before you rely on a site.

Does an existing gas system mean the mine is ready?

No. Gas collection, electricity generation and mining infrastructure are separate. An existing collection system may still need treatment, generators, electrical work and a gas agreement. Reuse depends on the equipment, its condition and your right to use it.

How are fees and capital estimates handled?

Search fees and deliverables are agreed for your brief before work starts. Preliminary capital ranges separate priced work from unresolved items; engineering, inspections and supplier quotes are needed to establish a build budget. Hardware, land and transaction costs are identified separately where relevant.
