The model

Stranded energy on one side.
Hashrate on the other.

Every day, energy gets thrown away because there is no economic way to move it to a buyer. Gas gets flared at the wellhead. Landfill methane gets vented. Wind and solar get curtailed when the grid cannot take them. That energy has no price because it has no customer.

Bitcoin mining is the one industrial load that can show up wherever the energy already is, buy every kilowatt-hour at a price that works, and switch off the moment somebody needs it more.

Energy with no buyer

Flare gas, landfill gas, curtailed renewables, and behind-the-meter surplus. Real energy, priced at or below zero because nothing is there to consume it.

A buyer that travels

We bring generation, containers, and machines to the energy, instead of waiting years for transmission to bring the energy to a market.

A load that can leave

Mining is fully interruptible. We curtail in seconds when the grid calls, when prices spike, or when a host needs the power back — no process to restart, nothing spoiled.

How we operate

Source, prove, energize, run.

The same four steps every time, whether the site is ours or yours.

Source

We screen candidate sites against our own database of flare volumes, landfill gas capture, industrial facilities, and environmental permit records before anyone drives out to look at one. Power availability, gas duration, land control, interconnect distance, and jurisdiction all get scored up front — so the sites that reach diligence are the ones that can actually be built.

Prove

Metered gas composition and volume over time, or a real load study behind the meter. We model each site across a full range of hashprice and energy-cost scenarios, not a single optimistic case. If a site only works at one bitcoin price, we do not build it.

Energize

Generation, switchgear, transformers, containers, and network go in as one package. Pads, permits, and interconnection are handled before hardware ships. Machines are racked, mapped, and pointed at your pool before a site is called live.

Run

Monitored around the clock from our own operations software — per-machine hashrate, temperature, power draw, and payouts, reconciled daily. Curtailment is automated against price and grid signals. You get the same numbers we do, every month, in a statement you can audit.
