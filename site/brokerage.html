04 / Before we begin

A few useful answers.

Does Proton buy my machines directly?

This service is brokerage: we help find a buyer and coordinate a potential transaction. You retain ownership until a sale is completed under the agreed terms. We do not purchase inventory through this service.

What equipment is a fit?

Buyer sourcing covers new or working used SHA-256 Bitcoin ASICs. Tell us whether you need new, used or either. Our used-equipment seller brokerage initially focuses on US business sellers; mixed-condition lots need more information before assessment.

Is there a fee if my equipment does not sell?

There is no upfront brokerage service fee. Our commission applies to a completed sale under your agreed engagement. Any separately agreed third-party inspection, shipping or other costs are distinct from the brokerage fee.

How is equipment condition verified?

For used miners, seller descriptions and supplied test records are the starting point. For new miners, provenance, warranty terms and the supplier’s authority to sell need verification. Buyer and seller agree any additional inspection or testing before closing. A listing or AI-assisted review is not a physical inspection, a warranty or proof of authorized-dealer status.

Can you source a specific model or a larger quantity?

Yes—that is the sourcing service we are preparing. We would confirm your exact requirements, research new or used lots from one or multiple suppliers, verify current availability and evidence, and present comparable options. We do not own that inventory or guarantee supply. For now, choose Finding ASICs to prepare a local brief; intake opens when the brokerage desk is ready.
