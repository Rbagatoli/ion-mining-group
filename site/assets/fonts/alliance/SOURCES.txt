Alliance typography: local Google Fonts files
Retrieved: 2026-09-22

The user-supplied Alliance website uses Bebas Neue for display text and DM Sans
for body text. These are the normal-style Latin subsets required by its first
two sections. The original source does not bundle font binaries locally.

Official stylesheet used to identify the files:
https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Bebas+Neue&family=DM+Sans:opsz,wght@9..40,300..600&display=swap
