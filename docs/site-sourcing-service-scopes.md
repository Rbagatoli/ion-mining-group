# Energy-site service scopes

These are scope templates, not accepted contracts or public fixed-price packages. Confirm the customer, geography, exact brief version, deliverables, completion date, effort cap, fee/payment basis, revision allowance, no-match outcome and exclusions before starting.

## Custom Site Search

Research new U.S. opportunities around the agreed usable load, delivered cost basis, operating flexibility and site capital limit. Record the buyer's known and rejected sites and exclude them from new discoveries. Deliver a ranked researched shortlist plus rejection reasons, evidence dates, infrastructure/reuse assumptions, remaining unpriced work, contact routes and next questions. Agree the treatment of a search with no suitable match; do not promise an arbitrary number of viable sites.

## Existing Site Review

Review the specific supplied opportunity, including one found by an owner, broker or referral partner. Retain the original source and investigate the target even when it is already known. Deliver a cost-basis reconciliation, infrastructure/gap review, evidence conflicts, hard mismatches, missing documents and next decision questions. Research completion does not establish owner interest, spare power, engineering feasibility or transaction completion.

## Submit or Refer a Site

Receive a private sale, lease, energy-supply or referral enquiry. Record one physical site and every distinct introduction route, original source, claimed authority, business contact route, last confirmation and any introduction/sharing terms. Verify authority and sharing permission before onward introductions. Do not require a Proton development partnership or promise a buyer, listing, fee or compensation arrangement by accepting a submission.

## Later qualification

Owner engagement requires its own authorized scope. Document interest, offered net kW/MW, delivery point, price/currency/basis, uptime and interruption terms, rights, infrastructure condition, funding responsibility and evidence dates. Independent review remains separate. Specialist costs, legal/engineering work, travel, construction, equipment and transaction execution are excluded unless the engagement specifically includes them.

Potential compensation or financial interests in an opportunity must be disclosed in the engagement. This software change establishes no referral commission or new commercial commitment. The internal $1,000 pilot is not a published price.
